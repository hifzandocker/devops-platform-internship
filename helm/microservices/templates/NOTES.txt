The frontend and backend microservices have been deployed.

To access the frontend locally, run:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "microservices.fullname" . }}-frontend 8080:8080

Then visit:
  http://127.0.0.1:8080/health

To access the backend locally, open another terminal and run:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "microservices.fullname" . }}-backend 8081:8081

Then visit:
  http://127.0.0.1:8081/health

Check deployment status with:
  kubectl get deployments,services
